Charging Station Near Me — questions drivers actually ask

How do I find the nearest charging station?

Use the live map above and tap the closest pin, or search your area name or PIN code in the box. Then verify availability in the operator app.

Are charging stations free to use?

Some are free (often at malls or showrooms), but most public stations bill per kWh. Filter for free stations if you want a no-cost top-up.

How long does charging take?

AC (Type 2) takes hours; DC fast (CCS2) usually takes 45–60 minutes for an 80% top-up on most Indian EVs.

What to check before you drive

A charger can be occupied, under maintenance, or behind a mall gate that closes at night. Before leaving: (1) confirm the connector matches your car, (2) check live status in the operator's app, (3) note the opening hours — many mall chargers shut with the mall.

Payments are almost always through the network's app or RFID card, and nearly all of them support UPI.
