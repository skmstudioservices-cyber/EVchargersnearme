Quick Charge Stations — questions drivers actually ask

What counts as quick charge stations?

A DC unit, usually 25 kW and above. AC (Type 2) is slower and meant for long parking.

How fast is fast in India?

City DC units are 25–60 kW; highway units 120–240 kW. Your car's onboard charger caps the real speed.

Which connector do these use?

Almost all Indian DC chargers use CCS2; a few older or imported cars use CHAdeMO or Bharat DC001.

Common problems with quick charge stations

⚠️ Claimed speed vs delivered speed

A 60 kW post can deliver 20 kW if the car, cable temperature or shared load caps it.

⚠️ The 80% taper

Charging slows sharply past 80% — on a trip it is usually faster to leave at 80%.

What to check before you drive

A charger can be occupied, under maintenance, or behind a mall gate that closes at night. Before leaving: (1) confirm the connector matches your car, (2) check live status in the operator's app, (3) note the opening hours — many mall chargers shut with the mall.

Payments are almost always through the network's app or RFID card, and nearly all of them support UPI.
