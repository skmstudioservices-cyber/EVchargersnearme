2. Filter to your plug

Most Indian EVs fast-charge on CCS2 and top up on Type 2. Filtering first saves you a wasted trip to the wrong connector.

Winter air quality where you are charging

From October onwards, air quality across north India changes fast. If you can charge at home or at a low-AQI location instead of an exposed kerbside bay, do that — it is better for you and for the grid.

Questions drivers actually ask

How do I find an EV charger near me in India?

Tap Near me on the map, or type your city, pincode or DIGIPIN. The map shows every public EV charger inside your chosen radius, with connector type, charging speed and tariff. You can filter to CCS2, Type 2 or CHAdeMO, and filter for wheelchair-accessible chargers.

How much does it cost to charge an EV in India?

Public charging in India typically costs ₹12 to ₹22 per kWh. Charging a 40 kWh battery therefore costs roughly ₹480 to ₹880. AC slow charging is usually cheaper than DC fast charging. Use the calculator on this page to estimate your own cost from battery size and tariff.

What is the difference between CCS2, Type 2 and CHAdeMO?

CCS2 is the DC fast-charging standard used by most Indian EVs and delivers 25 to 150 kW. Type 2 is the AC connector used for slower charging at 3.3 to 22 kW. CHAdeMO is an older DC standard still used by some Japanese models. Most Indian cars use CCS2 for fast charging and Type 2 for home and destination charging.

Are there wheelchair-accessible EV chargers in India?

Some are, and this map marks them. Use the accessible filter to show only stations that report step-free access to the charging bay and enough space to deploy a wheelchair. Accessibility data is crowd-verified, so please confirm a station with the one-tap card if you visit it.

How many public EV chargers are there in India?

India has roughly 25,000 public charging points across major cities and highway corridors, and the number is growing quickly. This directory lists verified stations in 40 cities, with the live count shown on the map.

Can I use this map offline?

Yes. Install it as an app from your browser menu. Saved stations, favourites and your last map position are stored on your device, so they load without a connection. Nearby stations refresh when you are back online.

About this map
