Free EV charging stations near me — the honest guide

Page last updated 29 September 2026

Free public charging exists in India, but it is rarer than forum posts suggest and shrinking every year. Here is where it genuinely survives.

Quick answerTruly free public charging is rare and mostly promotional: EV dealerships and showrooms, some hotels for guests, a few workplaces, and occasional new-operator launch offers. Free units are marked green on our live map. For daily driving, home charging remains the cheapest reliable option.

Where free charging actually exists

  • Dealerships and showrooms: many keep a free AC (and sometimes DC) charger for customers and test drives.
  • Hotels and resorts: often free for guests — ask at check-in.
  • Offices: some employers offer free workplace charging.
  • New-operator promos: networks occasionally waive fees when entering a city.

Why most “free” chargers disappear

Free charging is a marketing cost, not a business. As networks mature they move to paid per-kWh billing, and app updates quietly end free sessions. A charger that was free last quarter may charge ₹20+/kWh today.

The cheaper alternative to hunting free chargers

Home charging on a standard residential tariff works out to a fraction of public DC rates and is always available overnight. Public fast charging then becomes a road-trip tool, not a daily expense.

❓ Free EV charging stations near me — FAQ

Are there free EV charging stations in India?

Yes — mainly at dealerships, showrooms, some hotels and workplaces, and occasional operator promos. Free units on our map are marked green.

How do I find free charging stations near me?

Open our live map and look for green (free) pins, or filter your network app by price. Always confirm with the location — free status changes often.

Why do some chargers say free?

Usually a promotion by the operator or the property owner paying for it as a customer perk. These rarely last more than a few months.

Is free charging bad for my battery?

No — the electricity is the same. Free or paid makes no difference to the car.

What's the cheapest way to charge daily?

Home charging on your residential tariff, using public DC only for quick top-ups and highway trips.
