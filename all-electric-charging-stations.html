✅ What to actually expect in India

  • India's public chargers are mostly CCS2 (fast) and Type 2 (AC); CHAdeMO is older and rarer.
  • Highway fast-charging is concentrated on NH-48, NH-44 and the Delhi–Mumbai Expressway.
  • Many chargers are at malls, hotels and petrol pumps — often with a restroom and cafe nearby.
  • Cost is typically billed per kWh or per minute; apps vary by operator.

❓ Frequently asked questions

What connector do Indian EVs use?

Most new EVs use CCS2 for fast charging and Type 2 for AC charging; CHAdeMO is older and less common.

How do I find an EV charger near me?

Use the live map on this page, or search 'EV charger near me' on Google Maps and filter by connector.

Are highway EV chargers reliable?

Coverage is growing fast on NH-48, NH-44 and the Delhi–Mumbai Expressway, but always check recent reviews before relying on one.

How much does public EV charging cost?

It varies by operator and city — usually billed per kWh or per minute; check the app.

Can I charge at a petrol pump?

Many petrol pumps now host EV chargers, often with a cafe and clean washroom.

Do I need an app to charge?

Most operators use their own app or RFID card; some accept UPI at the point.
