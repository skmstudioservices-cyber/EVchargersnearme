2-wheeler EV charging stations near me — India guide

Page last updated 29 September 2026

Electric scooters and bikes charge differently from cars — most riders plug in at home and never visit a public station. Here is how charging actually works for two-wheeler EVs in India, and what to do when you are out of charge away from home.

Quick answerFor a two-wheeler EV in India: (1) charge at home overnight with the portable charger (normal 5A socket), (2) on the road use your brand’s fast-charge network — Ather Grid, Ola Hypercharger or TVS dealers, (3) if your model supports battery swapping, swap in minutes, (4) otherwise use any public point with your portable charger. 1 — Home charging: the default for two-wheelers

Nearly every Indian e-scooter and e-bike ships with a portable charger that plugs into a regular home socket. A full charge typically takes 5–8 hours, so the standard habit is to plug in overnight — exactly like a phone. This is by far the cheapest and most reliable way to run a two-wheeler EV.

2 — Ather Grid fast charging

Ather Energy runs Ather Grid, a network of fast chargers at Ather dealerships and partner locations across Indian cities. For Ather scooter owners it is the quickest top-up away from home — a short stop adds enough range to finish the day. Locate Grid points in the Ather app.

3 — Ola Hyperchargers and dealer charging

Ola Electric operates Hypercharger points for its scooters, and most brands — TVS (iQube), Bajaj (Chetak), Hero Vida and others — offer charging at their dealerships and experience centres. These are brand-specific: a TVS rider should head to a TVS dealer, not an Ola Hypercharger.

4 — Battery swapping

For compatible scooters, swapping beats waiting: operators such as Battery Smart, Sun Mobility and Honda Power Pack Energy run swap networks where a drained battery is exchanged for a charged one in a few minutes. Our battery swapping guide covers how it works.

5 — Public charging points with your portable charger

Most two-wheelers cannot use the big CCS2 car chargers — they carry their own portable charger. You can top up at any café, shop or public point that lets you plug into a normal socket, and at slow public charge points. ❓ 2-wheeler EV charging — FAQ

Where can I charge my electric scooter in India?

Mostly at home with the portable charger and a normal socket. On the road, use your brand's network (Ather Grid, Ola Hypercharger, TVS dealers), battery-swap stations if your model supports swapping, or any public point with your portable charger.

Can I charge my electric scooter at home?

Yes — most Indian e-scooters and e-bikes charge from a regular 5A home socket in about 5–8 hours. It is the cheapest and most common way to charge a two-wheeler EV.

Do Ather Grid or Ola Hyperchargers work for other brands?

Mostly no. Fast-charge networks are brand-specific — Ather Grid serves Ather scooters, Ola Hyperchargers serve Ola scooters. Other brands should carry their portable charger and use standard public points.

Is battery swapping available for two-wheelers?

Yes, for compatible models. Operators like Battery Smart, Sun Mobility and Honda Power Pack Energy run swap networks in several cities — swap a drained battery for a charged one in a few minutes.

How long does an electric scooter take to charge?

A full charge typically takes 5–8 hours on a home charger. Brand fast chargers can add meaningful range in under an hour, but daily home charging is the norm.
