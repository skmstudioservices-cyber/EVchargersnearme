EV charging networks in India — every major operator compared

Page last updated 29 September 2026

India crossed 29,000 public charging points in 2025, run by 80+ charge point operators. These are the ones you will actually encounter.

Quick answerIndia's major charging networks: Tata Power EZ Charge (one of the largest), Statiq (7,000+ chargers), ChargeZone (highway DC focus), Jio-bp Pulse, Bolt.Earth (1 lakh+ charge points listed), Ather Grid (two-wheelers) and PlugShare (community finder, not an operator). Each operator needs its own app; our live map shows chargers from all networks with the operator named on every pin.

How the pieces fit together

Each operator runs its own app with live status and payments — there is no single universal app yet. In practice, EV owners keep two or three network apps installed and use a live map like ours or PlugShare to see everything in one place.

India's public charging network passed 29,000 stations in August 2025, with roughly a thousand new installations a month — coverage is improving fastest on highways and in metros.

Picking the right app for you

  • City driving: the network with the most units near your usual routes — check our map by operator.
  • Highway trips: ChargeZone and Tata Power cover major corridors.
  • Two-wheelers: Ather Grid plus battery swapping for supported scooters.

❓ EV charging networks in India — FAQ

Which is the biggest EV charging network in India?

Tata Power EZ Charge is one of the largest single networks; Bolt.Earth aggregates over a lakh of charge points across operators.

Do all networks need separate apps?

Yes — each operator's app handles its own stations, live status and payments. Roaming between networks is improving but not universal yet.

Which network is best for highways?

ChargeZone focuses on highway fast-charging corridors; Tata Power also covers major intercity routes.

Which app shows all EV chargers in one place?

Our live map, community finders like PlugShare, and Google Maps show chargers from multiple networks — but live session status still requires the operator's own app.

Is public charging in India expensive?

Public DC fast charging typically costs more per kWh than home charging. See our EV charging cost guide for typical rates and per-km comparisons.
