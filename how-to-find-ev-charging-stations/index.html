How to find EV charging stations in India — 5 working methods

Page last updated 29 September 2026

There are more public chargers in India than ever, but finding one that is free, working and open is the real skill. Here are five methods, ranked by speed.

Quick answerThe five reliable methods: (1) our live charger map for 15 cities, (2) Google Maps, (3) network apps like Tata Power EZ Charge and Statiq for live status, (4) community finders like PlugShare, (5) your car's built-in navigation. Always verify live availability before driving to a charger.

Method 1 — live charger maps

Our homepage plots every mapped charging station for 15 Indian cities, colour-codes free chargers and gives DIGIPIN-precise directions from any pin.

Method 2 — Google Maps

Search “EV charging station near me” and Google Maps shows nearby stations with connector filters and reviews. Limitation: listings do not guarantee a working unit — always cross-check.

Method 3 — network apps

Tata Power EZ Charge, Statiq, ChargeZone and Jio-bp Pulse each show their own chargers' live status, let you start and stop sessions and pay by UPI. If you use one network often, install its app first.

Method 4 — community finders

PlugShare-style community maps show user-reported charger conditions and photos — useful for road trips and for checking whether a highway unit is actually alive.

Method 5 — your car's app

Most new Indian EVs bundle a station finder that knows your connector type and battery state — the fastest option from inside the car.

❓ How to find EV charging stations — FAQ

How do I find EV charging stations near me?

Use a live map like our homepage, search Google Maps, or open a network app such as Tata Power EZ Charge — and confirm live availability before you drive.

Is there one app for all EV chargers in India?

Not yet. Each operator has its own app, though roaming between networks is slowly improving. Community finders cover multiple networks but without live status.

How do I know a charger is actually working?

Check the operator's app for live status, or recent user reports on community maps. A map pin alone does not confirm a working charger.

Are EV charging stations free anywhere?

Most public charging is paid per kWh. Some dealerships, showrooms and hotels offer free promotional charging — our guide on free charging stations lists where to look.

What if the charger I reach is occupied or broken?

Always have a backup station within 2–3 km. Checking live status before leaving and keeping two options in mind is standard EV practice in India.
