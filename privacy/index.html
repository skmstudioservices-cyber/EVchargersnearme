Privacy policy

Last updated 29 September 2026 · plain English, no legalese

🧾 The short version

You need no account, we sell nothing, and we never ask for your name, phone or payment details. We only collect two things: anonymous visit statistics, and feedback you choose to give us. That's it.

📊 What we collect automatically

  • Anonymous usage statistics via Google Analytics (pages viewed, approximate city, device type). GA sets cookies; you can block them in your browser.
  • The page you are on and which site/link you came from, so we know which pages to improve.

🎤 The feedback popup (optional)

Occasionally a small popup asks what's missing on this site. If you answer:

  • Your answer (typed or voice-transcribed text) — only if you send it.
  • The page you were on and your browser type, to understand the context.
  • Skip it any time with the ✕ — nothing is sent unless you press "Send feedback".

We use this only to decide what to build next. We do not sell it, share it or use it for ads.

🗺 Map data

Charger/toilet locations come from OpenStreetMap (ODbL licence) — public community data, not personal data. Directions use your start point only for that trip and it is not stored.

🔐 What we never do

  • No accounts, no logins, no personal details.
  • No selling or renting of data, ever.
  • No advertising profiles, no third-party trackers beyond GA.
  • Voice answers stay on your device until you press Send — nothing records you without a tap.

🇮🇳 Your rights (DPDP Act 2023)

If you have shared feedback and want it deleted, or have any privacy question, email skmstudio.services@gmail.com and we will act on it promptly.
