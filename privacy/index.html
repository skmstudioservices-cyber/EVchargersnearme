Privacy policy

The short version: you need no account, we sell nothing, and we never ask for your name, phone or payment details. We only collect two things — anonymous visit statistics, and feedback you choose to give us.

What we collect automatically

  • Anonymous usage statistics via Google Analytics (pages viewed, approximate city, device type). GA sets cookies; you can block them in your browser.
  • The page you are on and which site or link you came from, so we know which pages to improve.

The feedback popup (optional)

Occasionally a small popup asks what is missing on this site. If you answer, we receive only what you send — your answer (typed or voice-transcribed text), the page you were on, and your browser type. Skip it any time with the ✕; nothing is sent unless you press Send feedback. We use it only to decide what to build next. We never sell it, share it, or use it for ads.

Map data

Charger locations come from OpenStreetMap (ODbL licence) — public community data, not personal data. Directions use your start point only for that trip; it is not stored.

What we never do

  • No accounts, no logins, no personal details.
  • No selling or renting of data, ever.
  • No advertising profiles, no third-party trackers beyond Google Analytics.
  • Voice answers stay on your device until you press Send — nothing records you without a tap.

Your rights (DPDP Act 2023)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 you may request access to, correction of, or erasure of any personal data you have submitted. Write to skmstudio.services@gmail.com and we will action it.
