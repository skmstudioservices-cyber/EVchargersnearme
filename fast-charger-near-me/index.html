Fast Charger Near Me — questions drivers actually ask

What counts as a fast charger?

Anything that delivers DC power — usually 25 kW and above. AC units (Type 2) are slower and meant for long parking.

How fast is 'fast' in India?

City DC units are typically 25–60 kW; expressway units run 120–240 kW. Your car's onboard charger caps the speed it can accept.

Which connector do fast chargers use?

Almost all Indian DC fast chargers use CCS2. A few older or imported cars use CHAdeMO or Bharat DC001.

Common problems with fast charger near me

⚠️ Claimed speed vs delivered speed

A 60 kW post can still deliver 20 kW if the car's charger, cable temperature or shared load caps it. Judge by the car, not the label.

⚠️ The 80% taper

Charging slows sharply past 80%. On a road trip it is usually faster to leave at 80% than to chase 100%.

What to check before you drive

A charger can be occupied, under maintenance, or behind a mall gate that closes at night. Before leaving: (1) confirm the connector matches your car, (2) check live status in the operator's app, (3) note the opening hours — many mall chargers shut with the mall.

Payments are almost always through the network's app or RFID card, and nearly all of them support UPI.
