Nearest Car Charging Station — questions drivers actually ask

How do I find a charger that fits my car?

Check the socket line on each pin — CCS2 (DC fast) and Type 2 (AC) cover most Indian EVs. Filter the map to your connector.

My car uses CHAdeMO — can I still find chargers?

Yes, but CHAdeMO points are rare in India. Filter for CHAdeMO and plan around the few that exist.

What if the nearest station is busy?

Keep a second option 2–3 km away. The operator app shows whether a unit is occupied before you leave.

What to check before you drive

A charger can be occupied, under maintenance, or behind a mall gate that closes at night. Before leaving: (1) confirm the connector matches your car, (2) check live status in the operator's app, (3) note the opening hours — many mall chargers shut with the mall.

Payments are almost always through the network's app or RFID card, and nearly all of them support UPI.
