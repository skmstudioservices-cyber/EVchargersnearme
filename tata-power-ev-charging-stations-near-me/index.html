About the Tata Power EZ Charge network

Tata Power was among the first Indian operators to build a national charging footprint — its EZ Charge network covers home, workplace, mall and highway locations. Charging is billed per kWh, and DC units cost more than AC.

Using the EZ Charge app

  • Sign up with your mobile number, add a payment method (UPI works).
  • The map shows nearby chargers with live status.
  • Start charging by scanning the charger's QR code.
  • RFID cards are available for regular users.

Tata Power home charging

Tata Power also installs home chargers for EV owners — usually a 7 kW AC wall unit. For most owners, overnight home charging is the cheapest and most convenient option, with public DC used for quick top-ups and road trips.

❓ Tata Power EV charging stations near me — FAQ

How many Tata Power charging stations are there?

Tata Power operates one of India's largest networks, with thousands of public charge points across cities and highways under the EZ Charge brand.

Can non-Tata EVs use Tata Power chargers?

Yes. Any EV with a matching connector — CCS2 for DC, Type 2 for AC — can charge at a Tata Power EZ Charge station.

Do I need the EZ Charge app to charge?

For most public units, yes — the app starts the session and handles payment by UPI, card or wallet. RFID cards are an alternative for regular users.

How much does Tata Power charging cost?

Charging is billed per kWh and varies by location, charger speed and state. DC fast charging costs more than AC — check the exact rate in the app before starting a session.

Does Tata Power install home chargers?

Yes — Tata Power offers home AC charger installation, which is usually the cheapest way to charge daily.
