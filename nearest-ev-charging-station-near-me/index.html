What to check before you drive

A charger can be occupied, under maintenance, or behind a mall gate that closes at night. Before leaving: (1) confirm the connector matches your car, (2) check live status in the operator's app, (3) note the opening hours — many mall chargers shut with the mall.

Payments are almost always through the network's app or RFID card, and nearly all of them support UPI.
