Highway EV charging stations in India — plan the trip, then drive

Page last updated 29 September 2026

Indian highways now have DC fast chargers on most major corridors — but road-tripping in an EV is still a planning exercise, not an improvisation.

Quick answerOn Indian highways, plan a DC fast-charging stop roughly every 200–250 km. Operators like ChargeZone, Tata Power and Statiq cover major corridors (Mumbai–Pune, Delhi–Jaipur, Delhi–Chandigarh and other golden-quadrilateral stretches). Always pick a backup charger near your primary stop and check live status in the network app before you drive.

Highway charging today

India's highway charging build-out has been fastest on the golden quadrilateral corridors and busy intercity routes, where fuel retailers and food plazas host DC fast chargers. Coverage thins on mountain and interior state highways — plan those routes leg by leg.

How to plan an EV road trip

  • Mark DC chargers every 200–250 km on your route in a network app.
  • Keep a backup station 5–10 km off each planned stop.
  • Check live status the morning of the drive.
  • Plan to arrive at 15–20% battery, charge to 80% and move on.
  • Carry the top two network apps with payment set up before the trip.

What highway charging costs

Highway DC units bill per kWh, generally at the upper end of public rates. On a typical EV, a 300-km day might need about 45–55 kWh — budget accordingly, and note food plazas make the 45-minute stops painless.

❓ Highway EV charging stations in India — FAQ

Are there EV chargers on Indian highways?

Yes — DC fast chargers run along major corridors like the golden quadrilateral, Mumbai–Pune, Delhi–Jaipur and Delhi–Chandigarh, mainly from ChargeZone, Tata Power and Statiq.

How far apart are highway chargers?

Plan for a stop every 200–250 km on well-covered corridors. On thinner routes the gaps are longer — always check your specific route before driving.

Can I drive Delhi to Mumbai in an EV?

Yes, with planning: mark DC chargers on the corridor, keep backup stations, and charge to 80% at each stop.

Which network covers highways best?

ChargeZone focuses on highway fast charging; Tata Power also covers major intercity routes.

What if the highway charger I reach is broken?

That is why you pick a backup station near every planned stop, and check live status in the network app before setting out each morning.
