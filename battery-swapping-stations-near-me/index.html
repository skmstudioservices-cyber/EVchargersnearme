Battery swapping stations near me — swap in minutes, not hours

Page last updated 29 September 2026

For electric two- and three-wheelers, swapping a depleted battery for a charged one takes under two minutes — no charging wait at all.

Quick answerBattery swapping replaces your empty battery with a fully charged one at an automated station, usually in under two minutes. It is mainly for electric scooters and rickshaws — operators like Battery Smart, Sun Mobility and Honda Power Pack Energy run swap networks in Indian cities, paid per swap by app.

How swapping works

Ride to a swap station, scan the QR code, and the cabinet releases a charged battery in exchange for your depleted one. Payment is per swap through the app — no parking while you charge.

Who offers swapping in India

  • Battery Smart: a fast-growing swap network across North Indian cities and beyond.
  • Sun Mobility: swapping for two- and three-wheelers, including fleet partnerships.
  • Honda Power Pack Energy: battery-sharing for electric rickshaws and two-wheelers.
  • Several OEMs bundle swap access with their scooters.

Swap vs charge — which is cheaper?

Per-swap pricing is built for gig riders who cannot park and charge. For a personal scooter charged at home, overnight AC charging is usually cheaper per km; swapping wins on time and for riders without home charging.

❓ Battery swapping stations near me — FAQ

What is battery swapping?

Exchanging a depleted battery for a charged one at a swap station — taking under two minutes instead of hours of charging.

Which vehicles support battery swapping?

Mainly electric scooters and three-wheeler rickshaws with removable batteries designed for swap networks.

How much does a swap cost?

Swapping is priced per swap or per kWh used, set by the operator and city — typically competitive with public fast charging for two-wheelers.

Is swapping faster than fast charging?

Yes — a swap takes under two minutes, versus 45–60+ minutes even on DC fast chargers.

Where are swapping stations concentrated?

In dense city centres and gig-riding hotspots first — check your operator's app map for coverage near you.
