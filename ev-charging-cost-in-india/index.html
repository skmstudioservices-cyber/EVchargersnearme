EV charging cost in India — what you actually pay per km

Page last updated 29 September 2026

Charging costs depend on where you plug in: home, public AC or public DC. The differences are large — and they decide whether an EV saves you money.

Quick answerPublic charging in India is billed per kWh. DC fast charging typically runs around ₹18–28 per kWh, public AC less, and home charging on a residential tariff is cheapest of all. A typical EV uses about 15–18 kWh per 100 km — so even on DC fast charging, running costs are a fraction of petrol.

Cost per km, worked example

A typical Indian EV consumes roughly 15–18 kWh per 100 km. At ₹22/kWh DC that is about ₹3.3–4 per km; on a home tariff it drops to roughly ₹1–1.5 per km. A petrol car doing 15 km/litre at ₷105/litre costs about ₷7 per km in fuel.

Why rates vary

Network, state electricity tariffs, charger speed and even time of day move the per-kWh price. Some states also run subsidised public chargers through their discoms — worth checking locally.

How to pay less

  • Charge at home by default; use DC only when in a hurry.
  • Some networks offer subscription plans with lower per-unit rates for regular users.
  • Off-peak charging, where offered, is cheaper.

❓ EV charging cost in India — FAQ

How much does it cost to charge an EV in India?

Public DC fast charging is typically around ₹18–28 per kWh depending on network, state and charger speed; home charging on a residential tariff is much cheaper per unit.

Is EV charging cheaper than petrol?

Yes, substantially. A typical EV costs roughly ₷1–4 per km in electricity, versus about ₷7 per km in fuel for a 15 km/litre petrol car.

Why is DC fast charging more expensive than AC?

DC hardware costs more to install and run, and its speed is a premium service — so operators charge more per kWh.

Do charging rates differ by state?

Yes — electricity tariffs and surcharges vary by state, and some discoms offer subsidised public charging.

Can I pay for charging with UPI?

Yes — nearly all network apps support UPI, cards and wallets.
