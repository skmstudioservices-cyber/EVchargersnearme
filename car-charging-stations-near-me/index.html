How to find car charging stations near you

  • Live map: our homepage shows every mapped charger for 15 Indian cities with connector types and directions via DIGIPIN.
  • Google Maps: search “car charging stations near me” — Google shows stations with reviews, and since 2019 it also lists thousands of Indian public facilities this way.
  • Network apps: Tata Power EZ Charge, Statiq and ChargeZone show real-time charger status and let you start charging from the app.
  • Your car's app: most new Indian EVs bundle a charging-station finder.

What to check before you drive

A charger can be occupied, under maintenance, or behind a mall gate that closes at night. Before leaving: (1) confirm the connector matches your car, (2) check live status in the operator's app, (3) note the opening hours — many mall chargers shut with the mall.

Payments are almost always through the network's app or RFID card, and nearly all of them support UPI.

❓ Car charging stations near me — FAQ

How do I find car charging stations near me?

Open our live map or search “car charging stations near me” on Google Maps, then confirm live availability in the network's own app (Tata Power EZ Charge, Statiq, ChargeZone) before driving there.

Do I need a membership to use a public charger?

Most networks let you pay per session through their app or an RFID card with UPI support. Some operators also offer subscription plans with lower per-unit rates.

Can I pay for charging with UPI?

Yes — almost every Indian charging network app supports UPI payments, along with cards and wallets.

Which connector does my car use?

Most Indian EVs use CCS2 for DC fast charging and Type 2 for AC. Some older or imported models use CHAdeMO or Bharat DC001.

Are public chargers occupied most of the time?

In busy city centres they can be. Checking live status in the network app and having a backup station within 2–3 km saves the trip.
