About EV Chargers Near Me

One live map, one clear answer, zero signup. Finding a working EV charger in an unfamiliar Indian city is still harder than it should be — scattered apps, stale listings, no clear answer on free vs paid or open-now. This site gives you a single map and a plain-English answer.

Why this site exists

Public charging information in India is fragmented across a dozen network apps, each showing only its own stations. We bring them onto one map with the details that actually matter — connector type, power, tariff, hours and accessibility — so you can decide before you drive.

How it works

Open data

Locations come from OpenStreetMap, the same community map used by millions, combined with verified network listings. Our map data is refreshed monthly.

DIGIPIN directions

Tap any pin for details, or use Near me for instant straight-line directions — no account, no key, works offline once cached.

Written for real trips

Guides for road trips, pay-and-use, free options, accessibility, and highway corridors — not generic filler.

Nothing to install

No account needed. Install as an app only if you want the offline experience.

Spot something wrong?

Maps improve through people like you. Tap any pin's report option, or email skmstudio.services@gmail.com — approved fixes appear after review. You can also edit OpenStreetMap directly; our map picks it up on the next refresh.

Contact

Questions, corrections, feedback: skmstudio.services@gmail.com. See our privacy policy for what we (barely) collect.
