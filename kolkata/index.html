Common questions about charging in Kolkata

How many EV chargers are in Kolkata?

2 verified public stations are listed here across 2 networks, and the number is growing.

What does charging cost in Kolkata?

Typically ₹15–₹22 per kWh for DC fast charging, cheaper for AC. The cheapest listed tariff here is ₹18/kWh.

Are there wheelchair-accessible chargers in Kolkata?

Yes — 1 station here report step-free access. Use the accessible filter on the map to show only those.

Which connector do I need in Kolkata?

Most Indian EVs fast-charge on CCS2 and top up on Type 2 AC. Some Japanese models use CHAdeMO. Filter the map to your connector before you drive.
