🗺 How to find EV charging stations nearby

The quickest method: search "EV charging station near me" on Google Maps. You'll see stations with locations and reviews. But before you drive there, check live availability in the network's app — chargers can be occupied, under maintenance, or behind a mall gate that closes at night.

Major charging networks in India each have their own app with live status and payments:

  • Tata Power EZ Charge — one of the largest networks, AC + DC across cities and highways
  • Statiq — fast-growing network of DC chargers
  • ChargeZone — highway-focused DC fast charging
  • Reliance bp Pulse — expanding network at Jio-bp outlets
  • Ather Grid / dealer networks — for two-wheelers

🔌 Charger types in plain language

Slow (AC, ~2–3 kW)

Your home wall socket — overnight charging, 8–12 hours for a typical EV.

Fast (AC, 7–22 kW)

Found at malls, offices and hotels. Adds meaningful range in 2–4 hours. Connector: Type 2.

Rapid (DC, 25–60 kW)

Highway and city fast chargers that charge a typical EV to 80% in about 45–60 minutes. Connector: CCS2 on most Indian cars (some older/imported models use CHAdeMO or Bharat DC001).

Rule of thumb: AC for where you park long, DC for when you're on the move.

💰 What does charging cost?

Public charging in India is billed per unit (kWh), and rates vary by network, state and charger speed. DC fast charging costs noticeably more than AC. Home charging remains by far the cheapest option for daily use.

Payments happen through each network's app or RFID card — almost all support UPI. Some state discoms also run subsidised public chargers.

❓ Ev charging stations in Kolkata — FAQ

How do I find a working EV charger near me?

Search 'EV charging station near me' on Google Maps, then confirm live availability in the network's app (Tata Power EZ Charge, Statiq, ChargeZone) before driving there.

What is the difference between AC and DC charging?

AC chargers (Type 2) are slower and best where you park for hours; DC fast chargers (CCS2) charge a typical EV to 80% in under an hour and are meant for quick stops.

Which apps show EV charging stations in India?

Tata Power EZ Charge, Statiq, ChargeZone and Google Maps together cover most public chargers in India.

How much does public EV charging cost in India?

Public charging is billed per kWh and varies by network, charger speed and state — DC fast charging costs more than AC. Home charging is the cheapest for daily use.
